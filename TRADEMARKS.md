## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your software works with, extends, or is based on
  MutinyBIM: "an add-on for MutinyBIM", "compatible with MutinyBIM", "a fork of
  MutinyBIM".
- Refer to MutinyBIM by name in articles, talks, courses, reviews and
  documentation.
- Use the unmodified repository, including its marks, to run and test
  MutinyBIM yourself.

Do this in plain text, without the logo, and in a way that does not suggest
PermitZIP made, endorses or supports your product.

## What needs written permission

- Using the logo, wordmark, icon or illustration in your own product,
  website, store listing or marketing.
- Naming a product, company, service, domain or package with "MutinyBIM" or a
  confusingly similar name.
- Offering a hosted service called MutinyBIM.

## Forks

You are welcome to fork the code. A fork you distribute or host for others
must:

- use a different name that is not confusingly similar;
- replace the logo, wordmark, icon and illustration with its own artwork
  (they are imported from `@permitzip/bim-ui`; swap the files and the brand
  slots in the theme);
- not present itself as the official MutinyBIM.

You may say "based on MutinyBIM".

## Questions

Ask in a GitHub issue or discussion before you use a mark in a way not listed
here.
